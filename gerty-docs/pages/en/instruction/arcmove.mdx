## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the [RobTarget](/en/datatype/robtarget/) or [Positioner RobTarget](/en/datatype/positioner-robtarget/) output of the weld path to **RobTargets**.
2. Connect [SeamData](/en/datatype/seamdata/) to **SeamData** and [WeldData](/en/datatype/welddata/) to **WeldData**.
3. Pick the motion type, speed and zone in the **Arc Move** tab, and choose whether the first target starts with `ArcLStart` (**ArcLStart**) and the last ends with `ArcLEnd` (**ArcLEnd**).
4. Put the **Instructions** output at its place in the instruction list connected to the **Instructions** input of [GERTY Core](/en/core/gerty-core/).

</Steps>
