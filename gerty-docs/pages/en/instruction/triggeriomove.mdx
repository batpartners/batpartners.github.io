## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the output of [RobTarget](/en/datatype/robtarget/) or [Positioner RobTarget](/en/datatype/positioner-robtarget/) to **RobTarget**.
2. Connect the [TriggIOs](/en/datatype/triggios/) output to **TriggIOs**; each trigger is defined with [IOEvent](/en/datatype/iodevent/).
3. Pick the motion type, speed and zone in the **TriggIOs Move** tab.
4. Put the **Instructions** output at its place in the instruction list connected to the **Instructions** input of [GERTY Core](/en/core/gerty-core/).

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  MoveL gives a `TriggLIOs` instruction, MoveJ a `TriggJIOs` instruction.
</Aside>
