A component that creates an ActUnit / DeactUnit instruction to activate or deactivate a mechanical unit. Use it to switch external-axis equipment (such as a positioner or track) on or off while the RAPID program runs. The target unit is named in **MechUnit**, and **Activate** chooses which instruction is created.

## How to use

<Steps>

1. Type the mechanical unit name (e.g. STN1) in **MechUnit** ((De)ActUnit tab).
2. With **Activate** on (`TRUE`) you get `ActUnit` (activate); off (`FALSE`) gives `DeactUnit` (deactivate).
3. Put the **Instruction** output at its place in the instruction list connected to the **Instructions** input of [GERTY Core](/en/core/gerty-core/).

</Steps>
