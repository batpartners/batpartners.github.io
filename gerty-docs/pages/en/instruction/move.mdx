## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the output of [RobTarget](/en/datatype/robtarget/) or [Positioner RobTarget](/en/datatype/positioner-robtarget/) to **RobTargets**.
2. Pick the motion type, speed and zone in the **Move** tab.
3. Turn on **MoveJ Start & End** for MoveJ on the first and last targets only, and **Fine Start & End** to stop exactly at them.
4. Put the **Instructions** output at its place in the instruction list connected to the **Instructions** input of [GERTY Core](/en/core/gerty-core/).

</Steps>
