## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the **GERTY Solver** output of [GERTY Core](/en/core/gerty-core/) to **GERTY Solver**.
2. Turn on **Simulation Pop-up** to open the simulation control window, and play the robot motion in the Rhino viewport from there.
3. Use the **Tool Center Plane** output for the current TCP plane.

</Steps>
