## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the output of [Scan ABB Controller](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) to **ABB Controller**.
2. Turn on **Gate** in the **Gate IO Systems** tab and pick the **IO Units** and **Signal Types**.
3. Use the names from **Signals** for the **Signal** input of [SetOutput](/en/instruction/setoutput/), [WaitDI](/en/instruction/waitdi/), [RealTime Display](/en/controller-utils/realtimedisplay/) and the like.

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  Connect to the controller with [ScanABBController](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) first; values are read only while connected.
</Aside>
