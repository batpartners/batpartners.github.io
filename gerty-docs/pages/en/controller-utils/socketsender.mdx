## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the **SocketBundle** output of [Code Solver (Option)](/en/core/code-solver-option/) to **SocketBundle**.
2. Turn on **Connect** in the **Connect** tab to open the socket to the controller.
3. If needed, adjust the queue limit, packets per tick, bundle size and interval in the **Queue/Timing** tab.
4. Turning on **Send** (Contorol tab) starts streaming; turning it off stops sending and pauses the robot. Press **Reset** to send again from the first packet.
5. Check the communication status and log in the **Log/Status** tab.

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="caution">
  After **Reset**, the robot stops and then moves again from the start position when it receives the first packet.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  Keep **Bundle Size** at the default 5, at most 8. A shorter **Interval(ms)** responds faster; a longer one lowers the load.
</Aside>
