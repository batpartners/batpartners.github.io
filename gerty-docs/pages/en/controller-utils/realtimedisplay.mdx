## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the **GERTY Robot** output of [ABB Robots](/en/robot-tool/abbrobot/) to **GERTY Robot** and the output of [Scan ABB Controller](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) to **ABB Controller**. To watch signals, connect their names to **Signal**.
2. Set **Interval(ms)** and turn on **Realtime Stream** in the **RealTime Stream** tab.
3. Read the current state from **Robot Angle**, **External Angle**, **CurrentPos** and **Signal Values**.

</Steps>
