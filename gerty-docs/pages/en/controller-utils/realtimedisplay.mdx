## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the **Robot Setup** output of [ABB Robots](/en/robot-tool/abbrobot/) to **GERTY Robot** and the output of [Scan ABB Controller](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) to **ABB Controller**. To watch signal values as well, connect their names to **Signal** (optional), e.g. the **Signals** output of [Scan IOSystems](/en/controller-utils/scaniosystems/).
2. Set the update interval **Interval(ms)** (milliseconds, default 50) and turn on **Realtime Stream** in the **RealTime Stream** tab.
3. Read the current state from **RobAx Angle**, **ExtAx Angle**, **TCP** and **Signal Values**.

</Steps>
