## How to use

<Steps>

1. In the **ABB Controller** tab, pick **Real** (physical controller) or **Virtual** (virtual controller) in **Controller Type** and press **Scan** to search.
2. Pick the controller in **Controller** (Connect tab) and turn on **Connect**.
3. Check the connected controller in the **Info** tab.
4. Connect the **ABB Controller** output to the **ABB Controller** input of the Controller Utils components and [Code Solver](/en/core/code-solver/).

</Steps>
