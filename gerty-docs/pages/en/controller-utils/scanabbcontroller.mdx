## How to use

<Steps>

1. Pick the **Controller Type** in the **ABB Controller** tab and press **Scan** to search.
2. Pick the controller in **Controller** (Connect tab) and turn on **Connect**.
3. Check the connected controller in the **Info** tab.
4. Connect the **ABB Controller** output to the **ABB Controller** input of the Controller components and [Code Solver](/en/core/code-solver/).

</Steps>
