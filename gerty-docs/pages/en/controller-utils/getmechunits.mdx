## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the output of [Scan ABB Controller](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) to **ABB Controller**.
2. Press **Update** in the **Mechanical Unit** tab to refresh the list, then pick the external mechanical unit in **MechUnit**.
3. Take the calibrated axis frames from **Joint Planes** and **Axis Names**. Check the axis setup and warnings (uncalibrated or locked axes, …) in **Info** and **Warnings**.

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  Connect to the controller with [ScanABBController](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) first; values are read only while connected.
</Aside>
