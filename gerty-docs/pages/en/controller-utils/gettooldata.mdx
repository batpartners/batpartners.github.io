## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the output of [Scan ABB Controller](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) to **ABB Controller**.
2. Connect the tool mounting base plane to **Tool Base**.
3. Connect the **TCP** and **Tool Load** outputs to the **TCP** and **ToolLoad** inputs of [ToolData](/en/datatype/tooldata/).

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  Connect to the controller with [ScanABBController](/en/controller-utils/scanabbcontroller/) first; values are read only while connected.
</Aside>
