## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the DataTree to split to **Tree**.
2. Connect the branch sequence index to split at to **Index**. It counts branches in order, not by path value.
3. Use **Tree A** (before Index) and **Tree B** (from Index on) separately downstream.

</Steps>
