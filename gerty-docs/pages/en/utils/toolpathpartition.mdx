## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the target plane list (branch) to split to **Targets**.
2. Set **Start(mm)**, **End(mm)** and **Substitution(%)** in the **Partition Settings** tab.
3. Connect the **Targets** output (split targets) to [RobTarget](/en/datatype/robtarget/), and split the instructions with **Index** / **Domain**.

</Steps>
