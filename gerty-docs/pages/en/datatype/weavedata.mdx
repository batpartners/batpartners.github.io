## How to use

<Steps>

1. Pick the weaving pattern (zigzag, V, triangle, circle) with **Weaving Shape** and the axis control with **Weaving Type**.
2. Set the pattern size with **Length(mm) / Width(mm) / Height(mm)**.
3. Connect the **WeaveData** output to the **WeaveData** input of [WeldData](/en/datatype/welddata/).

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  **Height(mm)** is used only by the V and triangle patterns; zigzag and circle ignore it.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
  What **Length(mm)** means depends on Weaving Type: the weaving frequency (Hz) for Rapid1/Rapid2, and the pattern spacing for the circle pattern.
</Aside>
