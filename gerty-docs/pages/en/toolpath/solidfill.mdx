## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the **GERTYShellData** output of [Solid Shell](/en/toolpath/solidshell/) to **GERTYShellData**.
2. Pick the internal and external infill patterns (**Int-Infill / Ext-Infill Type**) and the rotation (**Rotation(°)**) in the **Pattern** tab.
3. Set the number of top and bottom solid layers, the gap between shell and infill (**Layer Gap(mm)**) and the internal infill density (**Int-Infill Amount**) in the **Infill** tab.
4. Connect the **Deposition ToolPaths** output to [Deposition Builder](/en/toolpath/depositionbuilder/) or [WAAM Builder](/en/toolpath/waambuilder/).

</Steps>
