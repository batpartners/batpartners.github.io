This is a component that generates shell deposition toolpaths from a surface geometry (Brep or Mesh). You can set the layer height and bead width, the shell method (Contour or Zigzag weaving), target plane tilting, the per-layer direction, and the seam (start point) and wiping. For a large geometry, the work data can be exported in parts. Enabling `Use AntiOverhang` from the icon's right-click menu adds inputs for overhang reinforcement.

This page covers the form with <strong>Use AntiOverhang</strong> enabled from the icon's right-click menu, which adds the <strong>Overhang Density</strong> and <strong>Max Overhang Angle</strong> inputs.

## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the surface (Brep or Mesh) to **Model**. To reverse the direction per layer, connect a boolean pattern to **Dir Pattern**; to align the seams along a curve, connect **Seams**.
2. Align **Reference Plane** with the build plate (default WorldXY). To tilt targets around edges, connect **Edges**.
3. To control the overhang reinforcement, connect a density curve (Graph Mapper output) to **Overhang Density** and, if needed, **Max Overhang Angle** (default 90°).
4. In the **Slicing** tab, set the layer heights, **Tolerance** and the section method (**Section Proxy**).
5. In the **Shell** tab, choose the **Shell Type** and bead width (**Layer Width**). For Contour, use **Outline Shells**, **End Fillet Type** and **Path Order**; for Zigzag, use **Shell Thickness** and the **Zigzag** and **Relief** tabs to shape the weave.
6. Use the **Tilt** tab to tilt for overhangs or edges, and the **Seam** tab to adjust the start points (seam) and wiping.
7. Connect the **Shell ToolPaths** output to [Deposition Builder](/en/toolpath/depositionbuilder/) or [WAAM Builder](/en/toolpath/waambuilder/).

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="tip">
  For a large part, split the model into N parts with **Part Count** in the **Partition** tab and pick the part to slice (1–N) with **Part Number**, then export part by part.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
  Setting **Shell Type** to Zigzag shows the **Zigzag** and **Relief** tabs and the **Phase Pattern** input on the component, and hides **End Fillet Type** and **Outline Shells**.
</Aside>
