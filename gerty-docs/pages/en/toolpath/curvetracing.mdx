## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the curves to follow to **Curves** and the toolpath reference plane to **Reference Plane**. To tilt the TCP toward the surface normal, connect **Geometry** too.
2. Set **Tolerance**, the segment lengths (**MinEdge / MaxEdge(mm)**) and the tilt (**Tilting Weight**) in the **Settings** tab.
3. Connect the **Target Planes** output to the **Target Plane** input of [RobTarget](/en/datatype/robtarget/) to make robot targets.

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  With **Tilting Weight** 0.0 the TCP follows the Z direction of **Reference Plane**; with 1.0 it follows the Geometry surface normal (or the curve tangent).
</Aside>
