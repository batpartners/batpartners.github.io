## How to use

<Steps>

1. Connect the **GERTYShellData** output of [Solid Shell](/en/toolpath/solidshell/) to **GShell**.
2. To reverse the infill direction, connect TRUE to **Direction**.
3. Connect the **Infill ToolPaths** output to [Deposition Builder](/en/toolpath/depositionbuilder/) or [WAAM Builder](/en/toolpath/waambuilder/).

</Steps>
