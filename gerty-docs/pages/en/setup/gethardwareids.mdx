Hardware-based identifiers (such as MAC address, disk serial number, BIOS/UUID, and USB ID) are collected from the local machine for device identification. The device identifiers are processed locally, and the hardware IDs are neither transmitted externally nor stored.

## How to use

<Steps>

1. Place the component on the canvas; this PC's hardware IDs appear in **MachineIDs**, **USBInfos** and **USBIDs**. Press **Refresh** (Actions tab) to read them again.
2. To request a licence, press **Contact Support** to open the request page.

</Steps>

## Notes

<Aside type="note">
  The hardware IDs are processed locally only; they are not sent anywhere or stored.
</Aside>
